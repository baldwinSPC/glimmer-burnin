TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU POINT THIS AT HARDWARE

  1. This operator CORDONS the nodes it tests, immediately before it puts load
     on them, and releases them afterwards. Its footprint tracks
     maxConcurrentNodes rather than the size of your target list.

  2. A runner image is executed against real hardware, and the default images
     are NVIDIA-oriented. Set spec.runner.image per test to point at your own
     build for anything else.
